## 📝 Translation Principles Used

### **1. Cultural Appropriateness**
- Used proper Traditional Chinese terms for educational contexts
- Maintained formal, professional tone suitable for academic/religious education
- Preserved the warmth and pastoral care evident in the original

### **2. Technical Accuracy**
- **Knowledge Capture** → 知識擷取 (precise term for data/knowledge extraction)
- **Voice Cloning** → 聲音複製 (accurate technical translation)
- **Context Awareness** → 情境感知 (proper AI/ML terminology)
- **Wisdom Integration** → 智慧整合 (maintains biblical/philosophical connotation)
- **Legacy Preservation** → 傳承保存 (culturally resonant term for inheritance)

### **3. Educational Terminology**
- **Teaching Manifesto** → 教學宣言 (formal declaration)
- **Pedagogical philosophy** → 教學理念 (educational philosophy)
- **Response patterns** → 回應模式 (behavioral patterns)
- **Learning objectives** → 學習目標 (standard educational term)
- **Theological guardrails** → 神學護欄 (protective boundaries)

### **4. Biblical Context**
- Maintained references to Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Job in Chinese:
  - 箴言 (Proverbs)
  - 傳道書 (Ecclesiastes)
  - 約伯記 (Job)
- Preserved theological terminology appropriate for Chinese church context
