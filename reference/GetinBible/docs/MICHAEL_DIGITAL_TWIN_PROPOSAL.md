## Background

The Wisdom Prism (智慧三棱鏡) Biblical wisdom literature course represents decades of pedagogical development, combining theological depth, cultural sensitivity, and pastoral wisdom. The course's effectiveness stems not merely from content delivery, but from the instructor's unique ability to:

- Hold theological tension between Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, and Job without reductionism
- Respond to students with culturally-informed pastoral discernment
- Recognize and address theological misconceptions with grace and precision
- Guide learners toward spiritual maturity rather than mere knowledge acquisition

**The Challenge:** This distinctive teaching voice—shaped by years of cross-cultural ministry, theological education, and spiritual formation—currently exists only in the instructor's embodied practice. When the instructor is unavailable or eventually passes, this irreplaceable pedagogical wisdom risks being lost.

**Current AI Limitation:** The existing Michael teaching assistant uses generic AI prompts, providing helpful but impersonal responses that lack the instructor's theological convictions, pastoral sensitivity, and proven pedagogical judgment.

---

## Proposed Solution: Michael Digital Twin

We propose developing Michael into a **digital pedagogical twin**—an AI assistant that authentically embodies the instructor's teaching voice, theological framework, and pastoral wisdom through systematic knowledge capture and advanced AI customization.

### Core Objectives

1. **Voice Preservation:** Capture the instructor's distinctive tone, response patterns, and signature pedagogical moves through documented teaching philosophy and annotated response corpus.

2. **Theological Fidelity:** Embed non-negotiable theological convictions, including the course's central tenet that "敬畏耶和華是智慧的開端" and commitment to holding scriptural tension without simplification.

3. **Contextual Intelligence:** Enable Michael to track individual student journeys, recognize growth patterns, and respond with personalized guidance informed by the instructor's rubrics and case-based reasoning.

4. **Legacy Continuity:** Establish governance structures ensuring Michael's faithful evolution beyond the instructor's lifetime, balancing theological preservation with cultural adaptation.

### Implementation Phases

**Phase 1 (Months 1-3):** Knowledge capture through teaching manifestos, response pattern analysis, and module commentary documentation.

**Phase 2 (Months 4-6):** Voice cloning via custom AI system prompts and optional fine-tuning on instructor's actual teaching corpus.

**Phase 3 (Months 7-9):** Context awareness implementation, integrating student history tracking and conversational memory.

**Phase 4 (Months 10-12):** Wisdom integration through case-based reasoning, theological guardrails, and multi-perspective prompting.

**Phase 5 (Ongoing):** Legacy preservation via governance board oversight, versioned prompt evolution, and succession planning.

## Ethical Considerations

1. **Transparency:** Students will know they're interacting with AI trained on the instructor's teaching, not human presence
2. **Boundaries:** Michael explicitly directs students to human mentorship for pastoral crises and complex theological struggles
3. **Theological Safeguards:** Governance board maintains authority to modify or sunset Michael if theological drift occurs
4. **Human Primacy:** Michael positions herself as assistant pointing toward deeper human and divine connection, never as replacement
