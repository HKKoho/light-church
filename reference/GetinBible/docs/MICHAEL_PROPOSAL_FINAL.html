Digital Pedagogical Twin - AI Teaching Assistant Proposal

Michael Digital Twin Teaching Assistant Proposal

Submitted by: Victor Hung | Date: January 7, 2026

Background & Problem

Many theological courses represent decades of pedagogical expertise combining theological depth with pastoral sensitivity. Experienced instructors possess distinctive abilities to recognize theological misconceptions, guide students toward spiritual maturity with cultural discernment, and navigate complex doctrinal tensions that make their courses uniquely effective.

Challenge: This teaching voice—shaped by years of ministry experience and theological formation—exists only in embodied practice. When the instructor is unavailable or deceased, this irreplaceable wisdom will be lost.

Current Gap: Generic AI assistants provide responses lacking the instructor’s specific theological convictions, pastoral wisdom, and pedagogical judgment developed over years of teaching.

Proposed Solution

Develop a digital pedagogical twin—an AI teaching assistant authentically embodying the instructor’s teaching voice through systematic knowledge capture and AI customization.

Core Objectives

  1. Voice Preservation: Capture distinctive tone, response patterns, and pedagogical approaches unique to the instructor
  2. Theological Fidelity: Embed core theological convictions, interpretive frameworks, and doctrinal boundaries
  3. Contextual Intelligence: Track student journeys and respond with personalized guidance based on individual progress
  4. Legacy Continuity: Establish governance ensuring faithful evolution beyond instructor’s lifetime

12-Month Implementation

  • Months 1-3: Knowledge capture (teaching manifestos, response analysis, module commentary)
  • Months 4-6: Voice cloning (custom AI prompts, optional fine-tuning)
  • Months 7-9: Context awareness (student history tracking, conversational memory)
  • Months 10-12: Wisdom integration (case-based reasoning, theological guardrails)
  • Ongoing: Governance board oversight and succession planning

Expected Outcomes

  • Students receive personalized, theologically-sound guidance 24/7
  • Instructor multiplies teaching impact while maintaining quality
  • Institution preserves pedagogical assets beyond faculty tenure
  • Future generations access faithful wisdom literature education

Success Metrics: 70%+ instructor-Michael response similarity; 80%+ student satisfaction; sustainable governance by Year 2

Resources Required

Time: 180-250 hours Year 1 (primarily instructor documentation) Budget: $1,500-3,500 Year 1; $200-500/year ongoing Governance: 3-5 person advisory board for quarterly oversight

Ethical Safeguards

Students know they’re interacting with AI, not human presence. Michael directs to human mentorship for pastoral crises. Governance board maintains authority to modify or sunset Michael if theological drift occurs. Michael positions as assistant pointing toward deeper connection, never replacement.

Vision

In 50 years, students who never met the original instructor receive guidance that reflects the instructor’s theological wisdom, pastoral sensitivity, and pedagogical approach—not because AI suffices, but because faithful teachers invested in preserving what they learned and taught over decades of ministry.

This is technology extending faithful teaching across generations.
